Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a standard Generative AI course?

GenSpark offers standard curriculum starting points, but programs can also be customized around an organization's technology stack, use cases, learner profiles and capability gaps.

Who is this training designed for?

The program can be designed for software engineers, AI and data professionals, technical leads, architects, engineering managers and broader technology teams.

Can the curriculum be customized for our organization?

Yes. Curriculum scope, depth, projects and use cases can be adapted to the organization's requirements.

Is the training hands-on?

Yes. Training combines instructor-led technical sessions with practical coding and application, followed by instructor feedback. Roughly half of training time is teaching and coding sessions, with the remainder on hands-on work and feedback.

Can you train teams on our existing technology stack?

Yes. The curriculum can be aligned to the technologies, tools, frameworks and workflows relevant to the organization. GenSpark's training portfolio spans more than 100 technologies across application development, cloud, data and AI.

Can the program include our own business use cases?

Yes. Organization-specific use cases can be incorporated into the learning journey where appropriate.

How long does the training take?

Duration depends on the learning objectives, learner level, scope and depth of the curriculum. For example, GenSpark's Agentic AI Foundations & Practitioner curriculum is structured as a 10 to 12 week program.

How do we get started?

Share your team's goals, current capabilities and technology environment with our team. We'll help identify the appropriate learning path and determine where customization makes sense.
