The FDE Profile

Who is an FDE?

The market hasn't settled on one definition, but the demand is unambiguous: engineers who solve business problems directly in production.

01

Technical Fluency

Deliver reliable AI, cloud, and modern software architectures inside real enterprise constraints.

02

Problem Solving

Translate ambiguous business pain into crisp scopes, clean architectures, and workable code.

03

Client Acumen

Navigate client stakeholders, manage expectations, and map tech choices directly to ROI.

04

Build & Deploy

Own delivery end-to-end, from scaffolding and test harnesses to live production deployment.
