AI is raising the bar for SI delivery.

As AI accelerates software delivery, the bottleneck in systems integration has shifted from writing code to solving the right problem. Modern clients expect engineers who can sit with domain experts in the morning and push production-grade systems by the afternoon. That is the role of the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE).

FDEs connect the “why” to the “how.”

For SIs, the opportunity is not simply to hire FDEs. It is to build the capability at scale.

Strong engineers do not automatically become FDEs.

SIs have technical talent. The challenge is building engineers who can translate ambiguous client problems into solutions, work across unfamiliar systems, communicate with stakeholders, and own delivery through deployment.

01

Technical Talent ≠ FDE Readiness

Engineering depth is only the foundation. FDEs combine technical fluency with problem-solving, adaptability, and client context.

02

Training ≠ Deployment Readiness

FDE capability comes from applied, client-mirrored experience, where engineers scope, build, defend, and deliver under real-world constraints.

03

There Are No Ready-Made FDEs

The role is still emerging, so the talent pool is limited. Building FDE capability requires developing engineers for the role, not just recruiting them.

THE FDE PROFILE

Two FDE archetypes are emerging in the market.

Junior FDEs typically sit in the 0–5 year range, while Senior FDEs tend to have 5–10+ years of experience. The market currently skews toward the senior archetype, but FDE capability is not defined by tenure. With the right training and applied experience, it can be built across career stages.

The FDE skill set can be distilled into four core capability areas:

01

Technical Fluency

Apply AI, software, data, and cloud technologies to design solutions within real enterprise constraints.

02

Problem Solving

Translate business pain and ambiguity into clear, solvable problems and practical solutions.

03

Client Acumen

Understand business needs, navigate client environments, and connect technical solutions to real outcomes.

04

Build & Deploy

Build, deploy, monitor, and improve solutions in real production environments.

There’s no one-size-fits-all FDE. We work with you to define the archetype, capability mix, and talent model that best fits your business and delivery needs.
